Step 3: Define logical rules:
If X is father/mother of Y → X is the parent of Y.
If X and Y have the same father → X and Y are siblings.
If X and Y are siblings → they have a sibling relationship.
If X is parent of Z and Z is parent of Y → X is grandparent of Y.
Step 4: Load the program into SWI-Prolog.
Step 5: Enter a query to find a relationship.
Step 6: Prolog checks the given facts and applies the logical rules.
Step 7: Display the inferred family relationship.
